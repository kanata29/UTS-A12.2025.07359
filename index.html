    THE MATH BEHIND IT

    Know the numbers. Feel the ride.

    Two simple formulas power the planner. No black box, just a little arithmetic.

    Moving time

    distance ÷ avg speed

    30 km at 20 km/h is 1.5 hours of pedaling.

    Calories burned

    MET × kg × hours

    MET is how intense the ride is, picked from your speed.

    MET by pace
    • < 16 km/h Easy MET 4–6
    • 16–22 km/h Moderate MET 6–8
    • > 22 km/h Fast MET 10–12
